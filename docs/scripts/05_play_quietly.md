# 05 — Play Quietly With Me: cozy gameplay (30–45 min)

Downtime with company that isn't doomscrolling. Low-stakes game (farming sim,
exploration, building — nothing timed, nothing loud), commentary at campfire
density. Many viewers will have this on a second monitor — design for ears
first.

**[BED]** `python -m neurotune --mood deep_calm --minutes 45 --seed <ep#> --wav play_bed.wav`
(duck the bed under game audio; game audio itself kept soft — no jumpscares by
genre choice)

---

## COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:20

[CUE] Game already loaded, paused on something pretty. Cam small corner.

[SAY] "Evening. Tonight's a [GAME] night — nothing's chasing us, nothing
explodes. You can watch, or just leave me on while you wind down, tidy up,
whatever your hands need to do. Both count as hanging out."

## BODY — the campfire register

[CUE] Play genuinely. Commentary rules:
- One observation at a time, then space. ("There's the lake. Okay. Fish for a
  bit, I think.")
- Narrate intentions, not mechanics. ("I want the house done before winter" —
  never tutorial-speak.)
- Silences of 1–3 minutes are correct and expected. The game's soft loop +
  the bed carry it.
- One recurring bit per series — e.g. naming every animal after viewers'
  comments from last episode. Cheap, warm, communal.

[CUE] Mid-episode, once, look at cam:

[SAY] "If you're using this as background while you do a small annoying thing —
dishes, that one email — I see you. That's an honored tradition here."

## CLOSE — last 60–90 s

[SAY] "Good stopping point. The [farm/house/route] will be here next time, and
so will I. If you played alongside me, tell me what *you're* building. Sleep at
a reasonable hour. That's the whole outro."

[CUE] Save on camera. The save click is the end-card.

---

## Notes
- Game choice IS the accessibility feature: no flashing, no sudden audio, no
  fail-pressure. Check the game's photosensitivity reputation before featuring.
- Rights: confirm the game's streaming/monetization policy before recording
  (most cozy-genre publishers allow it; verify per title, note it in the video
  description).
- This format is the easiest to batch and the most forgiving to film tired —
  schedule it on low-energy days.
