# 01 — Cowork With Me: 4 × (25 + 5) Pomodoro session (~2 h)

**The flagship.** Daily anchor format ("Same Time Tomorrow"). Camera on you at
your desk, timer overlay on screen, mic muted during blocks.

**[BED]** `python -m neurotune --session 25x5x4 --work-mood steady_focus --break-mood deep_calm --seed <today's date as YYYYMMDD> --out cowork.mp4`
(use the rendered video as your timer + audio layer in OBS, your camera in a corner)

---

## COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:20

[CUE] You're already seated, already have your task list out. No intro animation —
the room *is* the brand. Soft chime as you look up.

[SAY] "Hey. If you've been circling something all day and can't land on it —
good, you're in the right place. We're doing four blocks of twenty-five minutes,
short breaks in between, and we start in about a minute. You only need one task."

(alt) "Hi. Don't tell me what you've been avoiding — just bring it. Four blocks,
short breaks, we start together in one minute."

## SET-UP — 0:20–1:00

[SAY] "Here's how this works, if you're new: I'll be quiet during the blocks —
muted, not gone. The timer's on screen. When it hits zero you'll hear a soft
chime, never anything sharp. Write your one task somewhere you can see it.
Mine today is: [YOUR REAL TASK — actually say it; specificity is the contract]."

[SAY] "Don't pick the whole mountain. Pick the next shovel of it. Ready?"

[CUE] Look down at your own work. Beat. "Timer's going... now." Mute.

## BLOCK 1 — 1:00–26:00

[CUE] Muted. steady_focus bed. You genuinely work — viewers can tell.
Posture changes, page turns, typing: that's the content.

## BREAK 1 — 26:00–31:00 (the breaks are where the personality lives)

[CUE] Soft chime. Unmute. Stretch *on camera* — model it, don't narrate it hard.

[SAY] "That's one. Stand up with me — even just for ten seconds. ... Water if
you've got it. If that block went sideways, that's fine. The block still
happened. You were here for it. Next one in a few minutes — you can swap tasks
or keep digging."

(alt themes for breaks 2 and 3 — one per break, keep each under a minute:)
- **Break 2 (halfway):** "Halfway. Whatever's done is more than was done two
  hours ago. Shake your hands out. Look at something far away for a second."
- **Break 3:** "Last break. The fourth block is the victory lap — pick the
  smallest possible piece so you end on a finish, not a cliff."

## BLOCKS 2–4

[CUE] Same shape. Mute during blocks. Do not add novelty — predictability is
the product.

## OUTRO — after block 4 (~2:00:00), 60–90 s

[SAY] "And that's the session. Four blocks. Whatever you got through — that
counts, and not in a participation-trophy way: starting is the actual hard part
and you did it four times. Leave one line in the comments: what you worked on.
I read them. Same time tomorrow."

[CUE] No hard cut — let the bed play 10 s over you tidying the desk. End card:
next session's day/time, no end-screen clutter.

---

## Notes
- **Live version:** identical shape; pin a comment with the block schedule, use
  chat for task declarations at the top.
- **What never goes in this script:** productivity guilt, "no excuses" talk,
  speed pressure, medical language.
- **If you had a bad take:** keep it. A yawn or a wrong-window fumble reads as
  presence, not error.
