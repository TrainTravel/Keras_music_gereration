# 04 — Get Ready With Me: morning routine / hair day (12–15 min)

Routine modeling. The job: make *starting yourself* feel doable by doing it
alongside them — hair, face, teeth, tidy collar, out the door. The hair-care
variant is its own quietly mesmerizing sub-format (your "watching someone do a
hair job" instinct is right — it's rhythmic, predictable, low-stakes intimacy).

**[BED]** `python -m neurotune --mood calm_focus --minutes 15 --seed <ep#> --wav grwm_bed.wav`
(morning energy variant: `--mood energize --tempo 96` — brighter but not pushy)

---

## COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:15

[CUE] Mirror framing — you in the mirror, camera over your shoulder, so the
viewer is standing next to you at the sink. Real morning light if you can.

[SAY] "Morning. Or whatever time it actually is — no judgment. Get up here with
me, we're getting ready. Doesn't matter for what. Sometimes the day needs you
dressed before it'll cooperate."

(alt, hair-day episode) "Hair day. This takes me twenty minutes and zero
willpower once I start — so let's start. Brush in hand. You too."

## BODY — 0:15–~12:00, narrated steps in real time

[CUE] Do the routine for real, at real speed. Narrate each step ONCE as you
begin it — a label, not a lecture. Long quiet stretches while you actually
brush/section/rinse are the ASMR core; keep the mic close enough to catch
brush strokes and water, gently.

Step labels (use as you go; trim to your real routine):
- "Face first. Cold water, thirty seconds, free reboot."
- "Teeth. Two minutes. I'll be boring together with you."
- (hair) "Sectioning. This is the bit that looks fussy and is actually the
  whole trick — everything after this is easy."
- (hair) "Brush from the ends, work up. Less fight that way."
- "Collar, shoulders, done-enough check in the mirror."

[CUE] One mid-routine line, once per episode, looking at the viewer in the
mirror:

[SAY] "If all you do today is this — washed face, done hair, real clothes —
that's a day that started. Some days that's the win and I mean it."

## CLOSE — last 60 s

[SAY] "Look at that. We're ready. For something. Go drink water, eat a thing —
and if your version of ready took longer or looked different, it still counts.
Same mirror tomorrow."

[CUE] End on the small ritual: lights off / door open / hair-tie on wrist —
pick ONE and keep it every episode (the series' signature beat).

---

## Notes
- No product-pushing, no perfection language ("fixing", "flaws"), no
  before/after framing. The routine is the content, not the transformation.
- This format batch-films well: three episodes in one morning with shirt/light
  changes.
