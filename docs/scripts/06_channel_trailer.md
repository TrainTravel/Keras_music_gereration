# 06 — Channel Trailer: "You're not doing it alone" (60–75 s)

The one video that explains the channel. Plays on the channel page and as the
pinned answer to "what is this?". Every line earns its place; no filler.

**[BED]** `python -m neurotune --mood calm_focus --minutes 2 --seed 1 --wav trailer_bed.wav`
**[CUE]** Cut between 2–3-second clips of each pillar as the lines name them.
Captions burned in. No flashing cuts — gentle 0.5 s crossfades.

## SCRIPT

[CUE] Open on the empty chair across your table. Hold one beat.

[SAY] "Some things are just easier with someone in the room. Starting the task.
Eating the meal. Getting ready. Even just... being in an evening."

[CUE] Cut to you working at the desk, timer visible.

[SAY] "This channel is that someone. We co-work in timed blocks — I'm quiet,
you're quiet, things get done."

[CUE] Cut: table set, two chairs framing.

[SAY] "We eat together, like people do."

[CUE] Cut: editor + terminal, ship moment from Build With Me.

[SAY] "Some nights we build things — and they have to run before we stop."

[CUE] Cut: mirror frame from GRWM; then cozy game save-click.

[SAY] "Some mornings we just get ready. Some evenings we wind down."

[CUE] Back to you, direct to camera. The only direct-address shot in the trailer.

[SAY] "If your brain works better with company — a lot of ours do — you've got a
seat here. No pressure, no productivity sermons, no pretending. Just: same time
tomorrow."

[CUE] End card: channel name + schedule grid (which pillar on which day).
Bed resolves on the root note (it will — the generator ends phrases on the
tonic).
