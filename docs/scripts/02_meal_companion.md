# 02 — Meal Companion: "Dinner for One (but not really)" (15–20 min)

Eating together, gently. The loneliest meal of the day is the target. This is
the most intimacy-forward format — and the one with the strictest guardrails.

**[BED]** `python -m neurotune --mood deep_calm --minutes 20 --seed <ep#> --wav meal_bed.wav`
(bed sits LOW — well under cutlery/room tone; it should disappear)

---

## COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:15

[CUE] Table set for one, camera across the table at seated-companion height —
the viewer's seat is *the other chair*. Food is normal, real, yours.

[SAY] "Hey — good timing. Sit down, I just made [FOOD]. You bring whatever
you've got. Cereal counts. We're just eating together."

(alt) "Pull up the chair. It's [FOOD] tonight. No agenda — just dinner with
company."

## SETTLING — 0:15–1:00

[SAY] "If you haven't eaten yet today, no judgment — that's half the reason this
exists. Get something, I'll wait. ... I'm going to talk a little, mostly not.
The quiet is fine. The quiet is the point."

[CUE] Start eating. Normal bites, normal pace. Room tone audible (cutlery,
chair, ambient) — gentle, NOT close-mic'd chewing.

## BODY — 1:00–~15:00

[CUE] Mostly comfortable silence over the bed. Every 3–4 minutes, ONE light
conversational line, low stakes, never a question that demands an answer:

- "This turned out better than last time. Lower heat, who knew."
- "It's been one of those days where the afternoon just... evaporated."
- "I keep meaning to eat earlier than this. We're working on it. Loosely."
- (mid-meal, once per episode:) "Drink some water. Yes, now. Thank you."

[CUE] If a long silence lands, let it. Do not fill.

## CLOSE — last 60–90 s

[SAY] "Okay — that's dinner. Thanks for eating with me; it's genuinely different
with company, even like this. If today's meal was your first one of the day,
tomorrow let's try for two. That's the whole goal. Same table next time."

[CUE] Start clearing the plate as the video ends — the routine completes on
camera.

---

## Guardrails (non-negotiable for this format)
- **Normal portion, normal pace.** This is anti-mukbang: no exaggerated
  quantities, no trigger-style close-mic chewing, no "finish everything" framing.
- **No body talk, no calorie talk, no "earned it" / "cheat meal" language —
  ever.** Many viewers have a hard relationship with food; we model *neutral,
  pleasant eating with company*.
- Plate visible but not fetishized; you eating > food porn shots.
- If sponsored food ever appears (later), disclose plainly at the top.
